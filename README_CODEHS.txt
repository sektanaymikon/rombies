THE NIGHT OF THE LIVING ROMBIES — CHAPTERS 1 + 2
October 9, 2026

START
Extract the ZIP first. Open index.html to play locally, or put all extracted
files into an HTML website project. Online Discover and publishing need an
internet connection and a hosted page. The CodeHS release uses a flat folder:
keep ALL its files beside index.html. No image folders or jsDelivr setup needed.
Do not paste the previous CodeHS-jsDelivr-index.html over this new index.html:
that older loader points to the Chapter One release.

INCLUDED
- Existing Chapter One, with a bridge to Chapter Two and Sandbox.
- Chapter Two's story, endings and evolution sheets: 924 in-game scenes.
- 31 replayable encounters: parries, dodges, team switching, domain QTEs,
  regenerating jackpot, timed survival, escaping, snacks, platforms and cover.
- Sandbox teams (4), waves (8), enemies per wave (8), obstacles (24).
- Editable names, descriptions, stats, AI, attacks, colors and artwork.
- PNG/JPEG/WebP uploads, automatic compression, local drafts, portable exports.
- Community Discover, publishing, bookmarks and share links through Firebase.
- Original generated-in-code music with volume control.

CONTROLS
A/D or arrows: move. W/up: jump. Shift: sprint. 1-4: attacks.
Space: charged ultimate. C: dodge. F: parry. Tab: switch. Esc: pause.
On-screen buttons support touch. QTEs show their own keys/buttons.
The story offers Previous, Next, Pause, speed settings and Next fight.
Story Assist reduces enemy damage. Losing an encounter can still continue
to the original story outcome. Replays do not change the campaign checkpoint.

SANDBOX
Choose a username on first entry. Start locally needs no account.
Create fight -> customize -> Playtest -> Save draft.
Artwork stays in this browser until publishing. Export fight includes local
artwork so you can import the fight on another device. Export your drafts
before clearing browser data. Chapter One and Two have separate save exports.
Use Account to connect a username and protect it with email/password before
moving to another browser. Anonymous accounts alone cannot be recovered after
their browser login is erased.

ART AND STORY
All 927 published slides and their click cues were reviewed. The in-game
story runs through slide 900; credits/evolutions continue through 924.
Source music links, extension/account instructions and sequel link slides
925-927 are not game scenes. The source decks and original game are preserved.
Sprites and backgrounds were deduplicated and compressed to WebP. The scene
renderer adapts the source positions, click groups, fades, fly-ins, zooms and
spins; it is not a pixel-identical PowerPoint renderer. Source commercial
music is replaced by the original procedural score.
