# Rotation, attack animations and custom fight music — October 9

## Editor changes

- Arena & obstacles now includes an always-visible Attacks & attack animations section. Select a player/enemy and wave; every regular attack and ultimate has separate resize/rotate buttons for attack artwork and its attack-time character animation. The normal character sprite is offered when no separate pose has been uploaded. Separate uploaded sprites and animated GIFs remain supported.
- Rotate through the green handle, rotation degrees field or ±15° buttons. Background handles at the top of the canvas stay reachable. Color-only previews now rotate too. Attack animations replace their owner's idle sprite in the preview, avoiding doubled artwork.
- Click-to-select, body dragging, corner resizing, mirroring and separate fight sizing are preserved. Enemy attack previews now use the enemy's actual default position.
- Fight music has its own sidebar tab. Upload MP3, OGG or WAV up to 8 MiB; use the audio preview, per-fight volume and loop controls. Drafts and portable exports include custom music. Published fights upload it to the existing R2 service. The game handles pause/resume, hidden tabs, cleanup and missing-track fallback.
- Normal play retains Chapter One fight presentation/fullscreen. Only builder Playtest uses the newer layout.

1. Extract Rombies-GitHub-fixes.zip. Upload all its files to the root of sektanaymikon/rombies, replacing matching filenames. Include all eight new customize-v5 scripts/styles. Keep existing images.

4. The Worker /health response should show maxMusicBytes: 8388608 and audio/mpeg, audio/ogg and audio/wav under supportedTypes. Test publishing a music-equipped fight on the hosted game after deployment.
