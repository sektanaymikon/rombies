## New Sandbox customization

- Your team / Enemies & waves: select 0–10 regular attacks per character.
  Keys 1–9 use those attacks; 0 uses attack 10. Space remains the ultimate.
  On-screen attack buttons follow the selected character's moveset.
- Disable Movement wiggle per character, for the entire fight, or in the
  battle pause menu. Chapter One also has a Movement wiggle setting.
- Characters, attack art, backgrounds, floors and obstacles support rotation,
  horizontal/vertical mirrors and opacity. In Arena & obstacles, drag the
  green handle to rotate the selected artwork; white corners resize it.
- Characters face their last horizontal movement. Set Original sprite points
  to match your source art; the Mirror buttons apply an additional flip.
  Face last movement direction can be disabled for a fixed orientation.
- The last arrow pressed sets player attack direction, even after release.
  A/D also set horizontal aim. Up/down touch aiming buttons are available.
  Disable Up arrow also jumps to aim up without jumping; W still jumps.
- Every attack behavior draws uploaded artwork, including melee, beams,
  bursts and support abilities. Thin images are fitted using their visible
  pixels. Adjust Attack art width/height, duration, muzzle offsets and aim
  angle; choose whether to show color effects and attack names alongside art.
- Each attack can use an optional Character sprite during this attack and a
  Character pose duration. It returns to the normal sprite when the pose ends.
- Fight box size changes the actual playable canvas, from 640 × 360 up to
  4096 × 2160. Numeric controls and the Arena box corner both resize it.
  Positions and scenery scale with the arena; character artwork keeps its size.
  Fullscreen fits the entire arena, including custom aspect ratios.
- Extra controls include sprite stretching, collision width/height, jump
  strength, stationary AI, health up to 20,000, optional names/health bars/
  floor auras, collision-box display and screen-shake settings.
- Settings survive local saves and portable fight export/import. Existing
  fights keep their original four-attack movesets unless you change them.

These controls require the updated game page/files. Firebase and R2 settings
do not need additional changes for this customization update. Nothing was
published to GitHub, CodeHS, Firebase or R2 by this update.

## Animated GIF uploads

- Every Sandbox artwork picker accepts GIF: backgrounds, characters, attacks,
  floor textures and obstacles. Choose "Upload image or GIF".
- GIFs keep their original frames, timing, loops and transparency in previews,
  editor canvases, battles, portable fight exports/imports and shared fights.
- Maximum GIF size is 2 MiB each. Use at most 500 frames, 4 million pixels per
  frame, and 4096 pixels per side. Very long animations with large frames may
  need shortening. Resize/shorten oversized GIFs before selecting them.
- PNG/JPEG/WebP images continue to be compressed automatically.
- To publish GIFs: Cloudflare > Workers & Pages > rombies-uploads > Edit code.
  Replace the Worker code with the updated rombies-uploads-worker.js supplied
  separately, then Deploy. Keep UPLOADS bound to rombies-sandbox and your
  existing ENABLE_UPLOADS=true variable. No Firebase changes are needed.
- The Worker health page now lists image/gif in supportedTypes after deployment.
  The updated Worker has been tested with mocked authentication and storage;
  it has not been deployed to your account by this update.
